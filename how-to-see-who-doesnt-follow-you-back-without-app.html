How to see who doesn't follow you back without an app

Quick answer: download your followers and following lists from Instagram's official data export, then compare them. Anyone in following but not in followers does not follow you back. Or use a web tool that needs no install and no password.

Method 1: official data export

In the Instagram app, open your account settings or Accounts Center and look for the option to download your information. Choose followers and following, pick JSON or HTML, and request the file. Instagram prepares it and sends a download link. Menu names can change, so search the settings for download your information.

Compare the two lists

Open the followers and following files and compare usernames. Anyone listed in following but missing from followers is a non-follower. For a few hundred accounts this works. For thousands it is slow and easy to get wrong.

Limits of the manual method

The export is a snapshot. It does not tell you who unfollowed you since last time, it needs a new request each time, and you must compare by hand or with a script.

Method 2: web checker

A web tool such as FollowLens takes a public username, compares followers and following and rechecks each non-follower. No install and no password. It works for public accounts only.

Which one to use

Use the export if you want full control and your account is private. Use a web checker if you want speed, bigger accounts or unfollow tracking between scans.

FAQ

Is the data export safe?

It is Instagram's own feature, so you do not share a password with anyone.

Can I see who unfollowed me from the export?

Only if you keep an older export and compare it with a new one.

Does FollowLens need an app?

No. It runs in the browser.
