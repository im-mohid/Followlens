How to see who doesn't follow you back on Instagram

The simplest definition is a list comparison: an account is a non-follow-back result when it appears in your following list but not in your followers list. You can compare lists yourself in your browser below, or use FollowLens to review supported accessible information.

Try a private list comparison in your browser

Paste usernames from your Following list into the first box and usernames from your Followers list into the second. Use one username per line. This small tool compares the text in your browser; it does not upload the pasted lists to FollowLens or send them to a server.

Your result will appear here. Nothing is sent to a server by this page's comparison tool.

How the comparison works

The tool normalizes usernames by removing a leading @, trimming spaces and comparing without case sensitivity. It then finds usernames that appear in Following but not in Followers. Duplicate entries are counted once. This is a text-list comparison, not a live connection to Instagram.

Method 1: Use FollowLens

  2. Enter a supported public username as requested.

Method 2: Use your own Instagram information export

You can request your information through Meta Accounts Center, then compare the follower and following data locally. This is a useful alternative when you prefer to work with an export you control. Read our no-app walkthrough.

Why a result can be incomplete

  • The follower or following list may not be fully available.
  • A username may have changed since an earlier export.
  • An account may be deactivated, blocked or temporarily unavailable.
  • The two lists may have been collected at different times.

Frequently asked questions

Does a non-follow-back result mean someone unfollowed me?

No. It means the account is in your following list but absent from the followers list you compared. They may never have followed you, or the data may be incomplete.

Can this page access my Instagram account?

No. The manual comparison tool only compares text you paste into the page. It does not log in to Instagram or fetch your account data.

Is the comparison tool private?

The comparison is performed locally in your browser by this page's JavaScript. Do not paste passwords, cookies or other credentials into either box.
