Who doesn't follow me back on Instagram?

Quick answer: compare the list of accounts you follow with the list of accounts that follow you. People on the first list but not the second do not follow you back. FollowLens does this automatically and rechecks each account.

What counts as a non-follower

A non-follower is an account you follow that does not follow you. A fan is the opposite: an account that follows you but you do not follow. Mutuals follow each other.

How to check step by step

Open FollowLens, enter a public username and start the scan. The tool compares followers with following, then rechecks every non-follower one by one so the list is not full of false results. Review the list and export CSV if you need it.

Why accounts don't follow back

Common reasons: they never noticed you, they follow only close friends, they are brands or celebrities, the account is inactive, or it is a bot. Not every non-follower means rejection.

What to do with the list

Keep accounts you value for content. Unfollow inactive or spam accounts to keep your feed clean. Do not mass unfollow in a short time, because Instagram can limit unusual activity.

Limits

Only public accounts can be scanned. Numbers reflect the moment of the scan, so rescan later for fresh data.
