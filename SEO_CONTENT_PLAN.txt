CONTENT STRATEGY
================

Primary clusters:
- Instagram followers/following analysis
- Engagement analysis and engagement rate calculator
- Account-quality/fake-account signals (with uncertainty)
- Follower changes between saved scans
- Trust, privacy, terms and responsible use

Avoid:
- Publishing many near-duplicate keyword pages.
- Repeating keywords unnaturally.
- Fake testimonials, ratings, user counts, or ranking claims.
- FAQ schema solely to chase rich results.
- Claims that a scan can prove intent or identity.

Build topical authority:
- Publish original tutorials based on the real app.
- Add authentic screenshots with alt text and captions.
- Explain methodology, limitations, update dates and authorship.
- Link related pages naturally.
- Collect genuine feedback and publish only permissioned testimonials.
- Update content when product behavior changes.

Ranking cannot be guaranteed by metadata, schema, or sitemap alone.
