SCAN HISTORY

Compare scans to review follower changes

FollowLens compares a saved follower snapshot with a later snapshot to show accounts that appear to have changed.

A baseline is required

Comparison requires an earlier scan. It cannot reconstruct changes that happened before the first snapshot.

What a missing account may mean

An account present earlier but absent later may have unfollowed, become unavailable, changed username, deactivated, blocked access, or been missed because of collection limitations.

Best practice

Use consistent scan settings where possible, allow scans to complete, and compare scan dates before interpreting differences.
