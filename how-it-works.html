How FollowLens works

FollowLens organizes available public follower and engagement information into useful comparisons.

Start a scan

Enter the public account details requested by the app. Review the instructions and scan scope before starting.

Compare followers and following

Review mutual follows and one-way relationships based on the lists returned at scan time.

Review engagement

Where supported and available, inspect likes and comments relative to follower count.

Compare saved scans

Run another scan later and compare snapshots to identify accounts that appear to have changed.

A scan is a snapshot, not a guarantee of complete or real-time data. A missing account is not always proof of an intentional unfollow.

What FollowLens cannot establish

  • Changes that happened before the first saved scan.
  • The reason an account is missing from a later snapshot.
  • Whether an account is definitively fake or controlled by a bot.
  • Information that is private, restricted, or unavailable to the scan.
