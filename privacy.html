Privacy Policy

Draft for operator review · Last updated 11 November 2026

Before publication, the operator must audit the deployed application, hosting, database, logs, analytics, cookies, and payment flow. Do not treat unverified statements below as a complete legal disclosure.

Information submitted

You may submit public Instagram usernames or other information requested by the app. Do not submit passwords, recovery codes, private messages, or sensitive personal information.

Password handling

FollowLens is designed not to request your Instagram password. This does not mean that no technical data is processed during a scan.

Scan data and logs

The operator must disclose what scan results, request metadata, and operational logs are stored, their storage location, access controls, and retention periods.

Cookies and analytics

The operator must list session/security cookies and any analytics or third-party tracking used by the deployed site and app.

Payments

The payment methods and providers actually used must be identified. The app should disclose what payment-related data is processed and by whom.

Retention and deletion

Publish accurate retention periods and a working request route for deletion or correction. These details must match databases, backups, logs, and payment records.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the contact route. Add a private contact method before launch.

Independent tool for understanding public follower relationships, engagement patterns, and changes between saved scans.
