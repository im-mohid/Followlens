Instagram unfollow tracker: see who unfollowed you

Quick answer: Instagram does not show a list of people who unfollowed you. To find them, save a scan of your followers, scan again later and compare. FollowLens shows new and lost followers between two scans.

How unfollow tracking works

Each scan saves your follower list. A later scan is compared with the earlier one. Accounts missing from the new scan are lost followers, and new accounts are new followers.

How often should you scan

Weekly is enough for most accounts. Scan after a post or campaign to see how it changed your follower count.

Why followers drop

Followers drop after spam cleanups, after you change your content, when accounts are deleted or when people unfollow. A small drop is normal.

Use it to improve content

If you lose followers after a certain type of post, test a different format. Pair the result with your engagement rate.

Privacy

FollowLens reads only public information and never asks for your login. Signed-in users can keep history between scans.

FAQ

Can I see who unfollowed me without scanning twice?

No. A second scan is needed to compare against.

Is it free?

The first 200 followers and 200 following are free. Larger accounts use credits from $3.

Will the person know I checked?

FollowLens reads public data and does not send notifications to anyone.
