Trust, transparency and limitations

Good analysis should explain what its data can—and cannot—tell you.

No password request

FollowLens is positioned not to ask for your Instagram password. Never share passwords or login codes.

Public-data scope

Results depend on data available to the scan. Private or restricted information may not be accessible.

Signals are not proof

Account-quality indicators are estimates and cannot establish identity or authenticity.

Snapshots have limits

Unfollower comparison needs an earlier and later scan and cannot recover older changes.

What can affect results?

  • Account privacy or availability changes.
  • Temporary platform limits, errors, or incomplete responses.
  • Username changes, deactivation, blocking, or other account status changes.
  • Different scan times or incomplete snapshots.
