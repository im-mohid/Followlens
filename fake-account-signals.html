RESPONSIBLE INTERPRETATION

Understanding account-quality signals

Observable patterns may help flag accounts for closer review, but no single signal proves an account is fake.

Why indicators are uncertain

Legitimate accounts may have low activity, unusual follower ratios, sparse profile details, or rapid changes. New accounts and niche communities can also look different from large established profiles.

Use indicators responsibly

  • Treat signals as prompts for review, not verdicts.
  • Consider the account's context and history.
  • Avoid public accusations based only on automated signals.
  • Do not assume a score reveals the person behind an account.
FollowLens does not claim to verify identity or definitively classify an account as fake.
