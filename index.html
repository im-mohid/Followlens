Instagram followers guide

Why non-followers matter

Accounts that never follow back or engage add little reach. Reviewing non-followers helps you clean your following list and keep active, real accounts.

How to calculate engagement rate

Engagement rate = (average likes + average comments per post) ÷ followers × 100. Compare it with accounts of similar size, not with huge creators. See our engagement rate note.

Signs of fake followers

Watch for accounts with no photo, usernames full of numbers, no posts, a huge following count and repeated spam comments. A sudden follower spike with flat likes is a common warning sign.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see who doesn't follow me back on Instagram?

Enter a public Instagram username in FollowLens. It compares followers and following, then rechecks each non-follower one by one to avoid false results.

How can I see who unfollowed me on Instagram?

Scan your account twice. FollowLens compares the two scans and shows new followers and lost followers.

Do I need to give my Instagram password?

No. FollowLens never asks for your password. It reads only public information, so your login stays private.

Is the Instagram non-followers checker free?

Yes. The first 200 followers and 200 following are free, plus 2 post analyses per day. Bigger accounts use credits from $3.

Can it detect fake followers and fake engagement?

It flags likers with no profile photo, number-heavy usernames and spam comments. It is an estimate, not proof.

Does it work for private accounts?

No. Only public profiles can be analyzed.

Is it safe for my account?

FollowLens does not ask for your login, so your account is not exposed by sharing credentials. Never give your password to any follower tool.

How accurate are the results?

Counts come from public data at scan time. If numbers look off, rescan later, because Instagram can limit what is visible.
