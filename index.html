Instagram non-followers checker: see who doesn't follow you back

FollowLens helps you compare accessible Instagram follower and following information, review accounts that may not follow you back, and understand changes between saved scans. Results are snapshots of information available at scan time—not a guarantee of complete or real-time coverage.

What does a non-followers checker do?

A non-followers checker compares two lists: accounts you follow and accounts that follow you. If a username appears in your following list but not in your followers list, it is a non-follow-back result for that comparison. It does not reveal why someone follows or does not follow you.

The comparison is only as complete as the source data. Instagram can change what is visible, limit automated requests, reorder results, or make some profiles unavailable. Treat a result as a dated snapshot and verify anything important inside Instagram.

What you can review with FollowLens

Non-follow-back comparison

Review accounts that appear in the accessible following data but not in the corresponding followers data.

Follower changes over time

Compare scans taken at different times. A first scan cannot reconstruct an unfollow that happened before tracking began.

Post engagement signals

Review supported public-post interactions as a simple context signal. Engagement is not a complete measure of audience quality.

How to use FollowLens

  1. Open the FollowLens app using the button on this page.
  2. Enter a supported public Instagram username as requested by the app.
  3. Start the scan and wait for the available information to load.
  4. Review the followers, following and non-follow-back results shown.
  5. Run another scan later if you want to compare changes over time.

Some accounts or list entries may not be available. If a scan is incomplete, do not interpret missing entries as proof that someone unfollowed you.

Accuracy and limitations

  • Public-data limits: private-account information and restricted data may not be available.
  • Rate limits: platform throttling or temporary errors can interrupt a scan.
  • Changing profiles: username changes, deactivation, blocking or account removal can affect list comparisons.
  • Timing: follower lists can change while a scan is running.
  • Intent: a non-follow-back result says nothing about a person's reason or relationship with you.

Free access and paid credits

The current app listing describes free access for the first 200 followers and 200 following, plus two post analyses per day. Larger scans use paid credits. Limits and prices can change, so the live app checkout or pricing screen is the source to check before purchase.

Want to compare lists without a third-party scan?

You can request your Instagram information through Meta Accounts Center and compare the follower and following files yourself. This gives you more control over the export, but the menu labels and export options can change. Read our step-by-step guide to checking without an app.

Frequently asked questions

Does FollowLens need my Instagram password?

No. FollowLens states that it does not request your Instagram password. Never share passwords, session cookies or authentication codes with third-party services.

Can I see who unfollowed me before my first scan?

Not from a first snapshot alone. You need an earlier follower list or a reliable historical record to compare with a later list.

Are non-follow-back results always 100% accurate?

No. Results depend on what information is accessible and complete at scan time. Platform restrictions, missing data and profile changes can affect comparisons.

Does FollowLens work with private accounts?

FollowLens can only work with information available to its supported workflow. It cannot guarantee access to private or restricted information.

Is FollowLens affiliated with Instagram?

No. FollowLens is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc.
