What is a non-followers checker?
A non-followers checker compares accounts in a following list with accounts in a followers list. The difference can help identify accounts that do not appear to follow back. Results depend on the data available at scan time.

Frequently asked questions

How can I see who doesn't follow me back?

Use a supported public username in FollowLens. The service compares accessible follower and following information and displays accounts that do not appear in both lists.

Can FollowLens show who unfollowed me?

Follower changes can be identified by comparing two saved scans. A first scan alone cannot establish who unfollowed you in the past.

Does FollowLens need my Instagram password?

No password is requested by FollowLens. Do not provide passwords, session cookies or verification codes to third-party tools.

Does it work with private profiles?

FollowLens is designed around publicly accessible information. Private-account information that is not publicly available cannot be reliably analyzed.

Can it identify fake followers?

It can highlight possible warning signs, but profile signals cannot prove that an account is fake.

Are scan results always complete?

No. Results reflect the information available at scan time. Platform restrictions, rate limits and profile changes can affect coverage.

Is FollowLens free?

There is a free tier with stated limits. Larger scans may require credits. Check the app for current pricing.
