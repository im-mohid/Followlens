Frequently asked questions

Does FollowLens need my Instagram password?

FollowLens is designed not to ask for your Instagram password. Never share passwords, recovery codes, or one-time login codes with third-party tools.

Can FollowLens show who unfollowed me?

It can compare saved follower snapshots and show accounts that appear to be missing from a later scan. It cannot reconstruct changes from before your first scan.

Can it prove an account is fake?

No. Account-quality signals are indicators only. They cannot prove identity, automation, or authenticity.

Does it work with private accounts?

FollowLens is described as using public data. Private or restricted information may not be available for analysis.

Is FollowLens an official Instagram product?

No. FollowLens is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.
