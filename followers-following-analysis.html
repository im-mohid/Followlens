FEATURE GUIDE

Instagram followers and following analysis

Compare available public followers and following data to understand mutual and one-way relationships.

What a comparison means

If an account appears in following but not followers at scan time, the relationship is one-way in that snapshot. If it appears in both lists, it is mutual in that snapshot.

Why results may differ

Lists can change during a scan. Privacy restrictions, platform errors, account deactivation, and username changes can affect returned data.

Interpret carefully

The comparison describes list membership; it does not explain why someone follows or does not follow an account.
